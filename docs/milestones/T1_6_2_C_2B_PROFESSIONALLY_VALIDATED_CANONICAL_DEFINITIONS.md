## Exact definitions

| Owner | Persisted definition text |
|---|---|
| `haircutting.elevation` | Elevation is the angle at which a selected hair strand or subsection is lifted and held relative to the shape/curvature of the head during haircutting. |
| `haircutting.section` | A section is a larger deliberately separated working area of the hair/head used to organize and execute the haircut. |
| `haircutting.subsection` | A subsection is a smaller working portion or strand selected from within a larger section for the actual cutting/work operation. Multiple subsections may be worked successively across a section. |
| `haircutting.subsectioning` | Subsectioning is the process of dividing/selecting smaller working subsections from within a larger section so they can be worked successively. |
| `haircutting.parting` | A parting is the line/separation used to divide sections or subsections. |
| `haircutting.projection` | Projection is the directional positioning of a selected strand in the direction or angular position in which it is intended to be worked or cut. |
| `haircutting.overdirection` | Overdirection is directing a selected strand away from its natural position/fall toward an established guide or cutting reference. It is used to create controlled differences in resulting length or shape after the hair returns to its natural position. |
| `haircutting.guide` | A haircutting guide is a previously established hair strand/reference that determines the cutting length/reference for subsequent hair. The guide provides the reference from which subsequent cutting continues. |
