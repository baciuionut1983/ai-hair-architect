Technique tutorials

Video + step-by-step

Dacă video nu pornește, deschide tutorialul direct: Balayage pe YouTube

Balayage / painted color

Create soft dimension by hand-painting lightener onto the lengths and ends, preserving the root area for a natural grow-out.

  1. Section the hair and identify the natural movement zones.
  2. Apply the product with a freehand motion, keeping the saturation lighter at the mid-lengths and ends.
  3. Process, rinse and tone to refine the final color result.

Dacă video nu pornește, deschide tutorialul direct: Root shadow pe YouTube

Root shadow / root blend

Blend the regrowth area softly while keeping a clean transition from root to lengths.

  1. Pre-lighten or prepare the regrowth area only if the client needs a lift.
  2. Apply a darker tone close to the root and fade it gradually toward the lengths.
  3. Process, rinse and finish with a shine treatment for a seamless result.

Dacă video nu pornește, deschide tutorialul direct: Precision bob pe YouTube

Precision bob

Create a clean outline with controlled shape, strong lines and a balanced silhouette for a polished finish.

  1. Create a precise center or side parting and establish the guideline.
  2. Work section by section, keeping the tension consistent and the line sharp.
  3. Finish with a detailed dry cut and refine the movement around the perimeter.

Dacă video nu pornește, deschide tutorialul direct: Layered cut pe YouTube

Layered cut / internal movement

Build shape through graduation and internal layering so the cut moves naturally and gives volume where needed.

  1. Establish the base line and the desired length distribution.
  2. Create vertical sections and distribute weight from the crown to the ends.
  3. Refine the internal shape with point cutting and check the silhouette from every angle.

Biblioteca Profesionala - Organizare pe Rubrici

Structura salon

1) Rubrica tunsori dama

Organizare de la simplu la avansat, pentru consultare rapida in lucru.

  • Tunsori scurte dama: pixie clasic, pixie texturat, bixie (bob + pixie), crop feminin.
  • Tunsori medii: bob clasic, bob drept, bob asimetric, long bob, french bob.
  • Tunsori moderne: wolf cut, butterfly cut, shag modern, mixuri stratificate.
  • Tunsori par lung: straturi lungi, U shape, V shape, framing fata, texturare varfuri.
  • Tunsori undercut dama: undercut discret, lateral, undercut creativ cu design.

2) Rubrica tunsori barbatesti

  • Toate tipurile de fade: low fade, mid fade, high fade, skin fade, drop fade, burst fade, taper fade.
  • Modele clasice: crew cut, buzz cut, side part, pompadour, quiff, slick back.
  • Modele moderne: crop texturat, french crop, mullet modern, faux hawk, fringe.
  • Combinatii: fade + barba, taper + textura, blend pe forme diferite ale capului.

3) Rubrica colorare (vopsit)

  • Tehnici de baza: vopsit total, vopsit radacina, refresh lungimi, neutralizare reflexe.
  • Analiza culorii: baza naturala, nivel, reflex, istoric chimic, tinta finala.
  • Tehnici avansate: balayage, folii/suvite, airtouch, babylights, root shadow, color melt.
  • Par blond: vopsit blond, tonare blond, corectare galben/portocaliu, mentenanta acasa.
  • Catalog coduri culori: structurare pe brand, serie naturala, cenusiu, auriu, cupru, roscat, violet, corectori.

4) Rubrica decolorarea parului

  • Explicatii: ce inseamna decolorarea, nivele de ridicare si risc structural.
  • Cand facem decolorare: pentru trecere la nuante mult mai deschise sau indepartare pigment puternic.
  • Cand evitam: fir foarte degradat, elasticitate slaba, scalp sensibil, istoric chimic agresiv.
  • Pentru ce culori e necesara: blonduri reci/foarte deschise, pasteluri, corectii de culoare dificile.
  • Pentru ce culori nu e necesara: inchidere de ton, reflexe apropiate de baza, refresh de nuanta.
  • Analiza firului: test suvita, porozitate, grosime, densitate, rezistenta la intindere.

5) Rubrica coafuri

  • Coafuri de ocazie/seara: coc elegant, coc texturat, hollywood waves, pony glam, impletituri elegante.
  • Coafuri de zi cu zi: brushing natural, beach waves, pony clean, half-up simplu, volum rapid la radacina.
  • Ghid de selectie: forma fetei, textura parului, rezistenta coafurii, timp de executie.

6) Rubrica extensii de par

  • Tipuri de extensii: clip-in, tape-in, keratina, micro-ring, weft/cusut.
  • Criterii de alegere: densitate par client, grosime fir, sensibilitate scalp, stil de viata.
  • Potrivire culoare: matching pe lungime, radacina si varf pentru blend natural.
  • Mentenanta: spalare corecta, periere, protectie termica si interval de intretinere.

7) Rubrica tratamente hidratare

  • Cu clatire: masca hidratanta, balsam intensiv, tratament nutritiv saptamanal.
  • Fara clatire: leave-in cream, spray hidratant, serum varfuri, protectie anti-frizz.
  • Cand se aplica: dupa sampon pe par prosopit, in functie de porozitate si grosime.
  • Plan hidratrare: evaluare porozitate, protocol pe 4-8 saptamani, reevaluare periodica.

8) Rubrica tratamente cu keratina

  • Tipuri de tratamente: keratina pentru netezire, keratina pentru reducere frizz, tratamente combinate.
  • Cand se recomanda: fir gros, poros, rebel, cu tendinta de electrizare si lipsa de control.
  • Cand evitam: par foarte degradat, scalp sensibil/iritat, incompatibilitate cu istoric chimic recent.
  • Protocol: analiza firului, test de suvita, aplicare controlata, sigilare termica, ingrijire post-tratament.
  • Intretinere: sampon fara sulfati, masca blanda, protectie termica si respectarea intervalului de refacere.

9) Rubrica spalarea parului, tipuri de par si sampoane

  • Tipuri de par: normal, uscat, gras, mixt, sensibil, vopsit/decolorat, cret, fin, gros.
  • Sampoane recomandate: pentru hidratare, volum, protectie culoare, scalp sensibil, detox.
  • Frecventa spalare: adaptata la scalp si rutina clientului, evitand supra-spalarea agresiva.
  • Tehnica corecta: dublu sampon bland, masaj scalp, clatire completa si temperatura moderata.

10) Produse de ingrijire dupa spalare

  • Produse recomandate: leave-in, protectie termica, crema anti-frizz, serum varfuri, spray volum.
  • Dozaj corect: aplicare pe lungimi/varfuri, fara incarcare la radacina.
  • Ordine aplicare: leave-in, protectie termica, produs de styling, finisaj.
  • Personalizare: selectie produs in functie de textura, porozitate si rezultat dorit.

11) Rubrica uscare si aranjare

  • Cum usuci parul: prosopare blanda, pre-uscare la flux mediu, sectiuni clare, directie corecta a aerului.
  • Cum il aranjezi: brush controlat, ondulare cu protectie, netezire pe suvite mici, fixare adaptata.
  • Reguli de protectie: temperatura potrivita, distanta corecta fata de fir, pauze pentru par sensibil.

Men's haircuts & style guides

Video demonstrations

Dacă video nu pornește, deschide tutorialul direct: Classic taper pe YouTube

Classic taper

A clean and timeless cut that creates a sharp transition from the sides to the top.

  1. Start with a strong sectioning line and a clear guide at the temple.
  2. Blend gradually from the shortest length at the sides to a longer top.
  3. Finish with a precise line-up and light texturizing on the crown.

Dacă video nu pornește, deschide tutorialul direct: Modern crop pe YouTube

Modern crop

A strong structured crop with volume on top and softer edges around the sides.

  1. Create a short, balanced outline around the perimeter.
  2. Leave more weight on top and remove excess bulk with scissor-over-comb work.
  3. Use a light finish with styling cream to create a clean, modern texture.
